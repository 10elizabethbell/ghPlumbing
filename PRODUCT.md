# GH Plumbing — product

- Platform: `web`
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking. Cal Sans from Google Fonts is the only external resource.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages, https://10elizabethbell.github.io/ghPlumbing/

## Users
- Homeowners in New Jersey with a plumbing problem (a slow toilet, a spraying showerhead, a leak), most arriving from a Facebook post on their phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser. (Inferred from where the business markets: a NJ home-improvement Facebook group.)
- The owner of GH Plumbing, who receives the site free and judges it first.

## Purpose
Turn a homeowner who saw a GH Plumbing tip on Facebook into a phone call. Give the business its first web presence (none existed as of 2026-10-21: no site, no Maps listing, no reviews).

## Positioning
The plumber who explains. Their own marketing is homeowner-tip infographics ("HOMEOWNER TIP", "SAVE THIS TIP") that tell people what's going on before selling anything. The site carries that: plain answers, then "Need plumbing help? Call GH Plumbing."
